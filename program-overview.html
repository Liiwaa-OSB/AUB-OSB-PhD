Program Overview

Scholarly Training

Developing Independent Scholars

Develop as a scholar. Contribute as a researcher. Lead through knowledge.

The PhD in Business Administration prepares the next generation of scholars, educators, and thought leaders in business. Through rigorous coursework, advanced research training, independent inquiry, and sustained faculty mentorship, students develop the methodological expertise, disciplinary depth, and intellectual independence required to make substantive contributions to business knowledge and practice.

From the beginning of the program, you will become active members of the OSB’s research community, working closely with faculty, participating in research seminars and workshops, and progressively developing your own research agenda.

The program encourages interdisciplinary inquiry and provides a strong foundation for scholarly publication, university teaching, international collaboration, and academic career development.

Graduates are prepared primarily for tenure-track faculty and research careers at leading universities, as well as research intensive roles in research institutes, policy organizations, international organizations, government agencies, consulting firms, and knowledge-intensive organizations.

Learning Outcomes

Program Learning Outcomes

Develop the skills to ask important questions, investigate them rigorously, and contribute original knowledge to your field.

By graduation, PhD candidates are expected to be able to:

Deep Theoretical & Empirical Knowledge

Demonstrate deep theoretical and empirical knowledge in their field of specialization.

Research Design & Methodology

Formulate important research questions and design rigorous studies using appropriate quantitative and/or qualitative methodologies.

Independent & Interdisciplinary Research

Conduct independent and interdisciplinary research that generates original contributions to business knowledge.

Advanced Analytical Techniques

Apply advanced analytical techniques, research tools, and evidence to address complex business questions.

Local, Regional & Global Implications

Evaluate the local, regional, and global implications of emerging business research and scientific developments.

Publication-Quality Research

Produce rigorous, original research suitable for publication in leading international peer-reviewed journals.

Effective Communication

Communicate research effectively through scholarly writing, conference presentations, teaching, and professional engagement.

Research Integrity & Ethics

Demonstrate research integrity, ethical responsibility, collaboration, and commitment to lifelong scholarly development.

Career Outcomes

Where Can Your PhD Take You?

Build a career around research, ideas, discovery, and teaching.

The program is designed primarily for candidates pursuing academic and research careers, while its advanced research and analytical training also prepares graduates for research-intensive roles across other sectors.

Career Pathways

Diverse Career Opportunities

Your doctoral training will prepare you to pursue careers where rigorous research, advanced analysis, and the creation of knowledge are central to your work.

Graduates will be prepared to compete for tenure-track faculty positions and research appointments at universities and research institutions internationally. The program’s advanced analytical and research training also supports careers in research institutes, international organizations, government agencies and policy institutions, consulting firms, knowledge-intensive organizations and research-intensive industry roles.

Academic Careers

Tenure-track faculty positions and research and university-based scholarly careers.

Research Careers

Research appointments in universities, research institutes, and knowledge-producing organizations.

Policy & International Organizations

Research-intensive roles in government, policy institutions, and international organizations.

Consulting & Industry

Research, analytics, and knowledge-intensive roles requiring advanced methodological expertise.
