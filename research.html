Research Areas

The PhD is offered as a PhD in Business Administration. Each student pursues one of three doctoral tracks while benefiting from interdisciplinary research opportunities across OSB and the wider university.

Business Analytics

Turn data into discovery and explore how analytics, AI, and emerging technologies can reshape business decisions.

Research areas include:

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Optimization and Operations Research
  • Supply Chain and Inventory Management
  • Revenue and Pricing Analytics
  • Decision Analytics
  • Big Data and Unstructured Data
  • Information Systems and Digital Transformation
  • Stochastic Modeling, Simulation, and Applied Analytics
Explore Business Analytics Faculty

Finance

Research the forces shaping financial markets, institutions, and decisions.

Research areas include:

  • Corporate Finance
  • Asset Pricing and Investments
  • Banking and Financial Intermediation
  • Corporate Governance
  • Financial Markets
  • Financial Econometrics
  • Financial Reporting and Capital Markets
  • Risk Management and Emerging Markets
Explore Finance Faculty

Management

What makes organizations work? Research it.

Research areas include:

  • Organizational Behavior and Organization Theory
  • Marketing, Management, Strategy, and Stakeholder Engagement
  • Leadership and Human Resource Management
  • Entrepreneurship and Innovation
  • Business Ethics, Sustainability and Business for Society
  • Careers, Gender, and Diversity
  • International and Cross-Cultural Management
  • Family Business and Small Business Management
Explore Management Faculty
