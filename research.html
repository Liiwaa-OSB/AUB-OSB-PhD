Research Areas

Three doctoral tracks, one research community

The PhD is offered as a PhD in Business Administration. Each student pursues one of three doctoral tracks while benefiting from interdisciplinary research opportunities across OSB and the wider university.

Business Analytics Finance Management

  • Optimization and Operations Research
  • Supply Chain and Inventory Management
  • Revenue and Pricing Analytics
  • Decision Analytics
  • Big Data and Unstructured Data
  • Information Systems and Digital Transformation

Beyond a Single Track

Interdisciplinary by design

While each student is admitted into one doctoral track, OSB actively encourages research that crosses disciplinary boundaries — between analytics and finance, management and technology, or business and the wider social sciences. Research seminars, workshops, and faculty collaboration are open across all three tracks.
