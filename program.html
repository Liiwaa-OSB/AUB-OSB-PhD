Degree & Candidacy

Milestones along the way

  • A program of study tailored to the student's track and research interests.
  • Advanced coursework in research methods and the chosen specialization.
  • Qualifying Exam Part I — a comprehensive examination of the student's field and related areas.
  • Qualifying Exam Part II — an oral defense of the doctoral thesis proposal.
  • Original dissertation research, supervised and reviewed by a doctoral thesis committee.
  • Successful defense of a dissertation that makes an original scholarly contribution.
  • Completion of AUB-wide doctoral requirements, including teaching preparation and residence/academic-standing requirements.
Beyond the Classroom

Mentorship, teaching, and a global network

Research Mentoring & Community

Academic advising in the early stage evolves into work with a thesis adviser and doctoral thesis committee. Students are active members of OSB's scholarly community through research seminars, workshops, visiting-scholar activities, and conference participation.

Teaching & Career Development

Doctoral students complete AUB's required preparation in teaching in higher education and gain supervised teaching experience through graduate assistantships — alongside publication training, research ethics, and job-market preparation.

International Research Exposure

Students build scholarly networks through conferences, collaborative research, and engagement with visiting and partner scholars — with the possibility of a semester at a partner institution, subject to academic approval and partnership arrangements.
