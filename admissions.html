Admissions & Financial Support

Admissions

Are you ready to turn your research interests into original scholarship?

Show us the questions that motivate you, the research you hope to pursue, and the scholar you aspire to become.

A strong application should show more than academic achievement; it should show the research questions you want to pursue, why they matter, and why OSB is the right intellectual environment for them.

Admission to the PhD in Business Administration is highly selective.

We seek outstanding applicants with strong academic achievement, intellectual curiosity, analytical ability, research motivation, and the potential to develop into independent scholars.

Applications are assessed holistically based on academic preparation, research potential, fit with OSB faculty expertise, and readiness for doctoral study. Applicants must satisfy the general admission requirements of the American University of Beirut (AUB) Graduate Council in addition to the program-specific requirements.

Eligibility

Eligibility

Your academic background is the starting point; your research potential and readiness for doctoral study are essential.

Applicants should meet the following requirements:

A bachelor’s and master’s degree in business administration or a related discipline from a recognized institution for the regular track.

A minimum GPA of 3.7/4.0, or equivalent, in the most recent degree, subject to AUB and program admissions policies.

Competitive GMAT or GRE scores obtained within the period accepted by AUB/program regulations.

English-language proficiency meeting AUB requirements.

Evidence of research potential, intellectual maturity, and alignment with OSB’s research strengths.

Who Should Apply

Who Should Apply

Applicants may come from business and related fields including economics, finance, accounting, management, marketing, business analytics, operations research, engineering, computer science, statistics, mathematics, information systems, health sciences, and other relevant disciplines. Applicants from other fields, within what is allowed by the Ministry of Higher Education, may also be considered when they demonstrate strong potential for doctoral research. Preparatory coursework may be required on a case-by-case basis and does not count toward degree requirements.

Application

Application Materials

Show us the questions that motivate you, the research you hope to pursue, and the scholar you aspire to become.

Applications are submitted through the AUB online graduate application system and should include:

Online AUB graduate application

Complete the official AUB graduate application form.

Official transcripts

From all postsecondary institutions attended.

Curriculum vitae

Highlighting education, research experience, and professional background.

Statement of purpose

Explaining academic background, research interests, and motivation for doctoral study.

Research statement (2–4 pages)

Describing the proposed research area, its significance, and potential methodological approach.

Academic writing sample

A thesis chapter, research paper, working paper, or publication.

Three academic letters of recommendation

Addressing the applicant’s research potential and readiness for doctoral study.

Official GMAT or GRE scores

And proof of English-language proficiency, as required.

Interview: Shortlisted applicants will be invited for an interview with the admissions committee.

Website note: Link the application deadline, English-language requirements, and official application portal directly to AUB Admissions so that time-sensitive university requirements remain current.

Funding

Financial Support

Focus on your research and development as a scholar.

The PhD in Business Administration is planned as a fully funded, full-time doctoral program.

In line with AUB doctoral funding arrangements and program requirements, admitted students are expected to receive:

Tuition Support

Tuition support is expected to be provided as part of the doctoral funding arrangement.

Monthly Stipend

A monthly stipend may be provided in connection with graduate teaching and/or research responsibilities, subject to satisfactory academic progress and the terms of the applicable doctoral appointment.

Additional support: Competitive support may be available for conference participation, research activities, and approved international research experiences.
