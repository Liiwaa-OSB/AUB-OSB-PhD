Admissions

Admission to the PhD in Business Administration is highly selective. The program seeks outstanding applicants with exceptional academic achievement, intellectual curiosity, strong analytical ability, research motivation, and clear potential to develop into independent scholars. Decisions are based on a holistic assessment of academic preparation, research potential, fit with OSB’s areas of faculty expertise, and readiness for doctoral study. Applicants must satisfy the general admission requirements of the American University of Beirut (AUB) Graduate Council in addition to the program-specific requirements.

Eligibility

Applicants should meet the following requirements:

  • A bachelor’s and master’s degree in business administration or a related discipline from a recognized institution for the regular track.

  • A minimum GPA of 3.7/4.0, or equivalent, in the most recent degree, subject to AUB and program admissions policies.

  • Competitive GMAT or GRE scores obtained within the period accepted by AUB/program regulations.

  • English-language proficiency meeting AUB requirements.

  • Evidence of research potential, intellectual maturity, and strong alignment with OSB’s research strengths.

Who Should Apply

Applicants may come from business and related fields including economics, finance, accounting, management, marketing, business analytics, operations research, engineering, computer science, statistics, mathematics, information systems, health sciences, and other relevant disciplines. Applicants from other fields, within what is allowed by the Ministry of Higher Education, may also be considered when they demonstrate strong potential for doctoral research. Preparatory coursework may be required on a case-by-case basis and does not count toward degree requirements.

Application Materials

Applications are submitted through the AUB online graduate application system and should include:

  • Complete the official AUB graduate application form.

  • Official transcripts from all postsecondary institutions attended.

  • Curriculum vitae highlighting education, research experience, publications or presentations, teaching, professional experience, awards, and scholarships.

  • Statement of purpose explaining academic background, research interests, motivation for doctoral study, career goals, and fit with OSB faculty expertise.

  • Research statement (2–4 pages) describing the proposed research area, its significance, and potential methodological approach.

  • Academic writing sample — a thesis chapter, research paper, working paper, or publication.

  • Three academic letters of recommendation addressing the applicant’s research potential and readiness for doctoral study.

  • Official GMAT or GRE scores, and proof of English-language proficiency, as required.

Interview: Shortlisted applicants will be invited for an interview with the admissions committee.

Financial Support

The PhD in Business Administration is planned as a fully funded, full-time doctoral program. In line with AUB doctoral funding arrangements and program requirements, admitted students are expected to receive:

Tuition Support

Tuition support is expected to be provided as part of the doctoral funding arrangement.

Monthly Stipend

A monthly stipend may be provided in connection with graduate teaching and/or research responsibilities, subject to satisfactory academic progress and the terms of the applicable doctoral appointment.

Additional support

Additional competitive support may be available for conference participation, research activities, and approved international research experiences.
