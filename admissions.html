Admissions — Fall 2027

A highly selective, fully funded doctoral program

Admission is based on a holistic assessment of academic preparation, research potential, fit with OSB's areas of faculty expertise, and readiness for doctoral study. Applicants must satisfy the general admission requirements of the AUB Graduate Council in addition to the program-specific requirements below.

Eligibility

Who should apply

The program seeks outstanding applicants with exceptional academic achievement, intellectual curiosity, strong analytical ability, research motivation, and clear potential to develop into independent scholars.

Applicants may come from business and related fields — economics, finance, accounting, management, marketing, business analytics, operations research, engineering, computer science, statistics, mathematics, information systems, health sciences, and other relevant disciplines. Applicants from other fields may also be considered when they demonstrate strong potential for doctoral research; preparatory coursework may be required on a case-by-case basis and does not count toward degree requirements.

  • A bachelor's and master's degree in business administration or a related discipline for the regular track. Exceptionally qualified bachelor's-degree holders may be considered for the accelerated track.
  • A minimum GPA of 3.7/4.0, or equivalent, in the most recent degree — exceptional candidates with outstanding research potential may be considered even if slightly below this threshold.
  • Competitive GMAT or GRE scores, obtained within the period accepted by AUB and program regulations.
  • English-language proficiency meeting AUB requirements.
  • Evidence of research potential, intellectual maturity, and strong alignment with the research strengths of OSB.
How to Apply

Application materials

Applications are submitted through the AUB online graduate application system and should include:

  1. Online AUB graduate applicationSubmitted through the official AUB graduate application system.

  2. Official transcriptsFrom all postsecondary institutions attended.

  3. Curriculum vitaeHighlighting education, research experience, publications or presentations, teaching, professional experience, awards, and scholarships.

  4. Statement of purposeExplaining academic background, research interests, motivation for doctoral study, career goals, and fit with OSB faculty expertise.

  5. Research statement (2–4 pages)Describing the proposed research area, its significance, preliminary questions, and potential methodological approach.

  6. Academic writing sampleSuch as a thesis chapter, research paper, working paper, or publication.

  7. Three academic letters of recommendationAddressing the applicant's research potential and readiness for doctoral study.

  8. Official GMAT or GRE scoresAnd proof of English-language proficiency, as required.

Shortlisted applicants will be invited for an interview, during which they will present their proposed research and discuss their academic interests with the admissions committee.

Application deadlines, English-language requirements, and the official application portal are maintained by AUB Graduate Admissions and should always be confirmed there directly.

Financial Support

A fully funded doctoral program

The PhD in Business Administration is planned as a fully funded, full-time doctoral program. In line with AUB doctoral funding arrangements and program requirements, admitted students are expected to receive tuition support and a monthly stipend in connection with graduate teaching and/or research responsibilities, subject to satisfactory academic progress and the terms of the applicable doctoral appointment.

Additional competitive support may be available for conference participation, research activities, and approved international research experiences.
