Admissions

Admissions

Admissions

Admission to the PhD in Business Administration is highly selective. The program seeks outstanding applicants with exceptional academic achievement, intellectual curiosity, strong analytical ability, research motivation, and clear potential to develop into independent scholars. Decisions are based on a holistic assessment of academic preparation, research potential, fit with OSB’s areas of faculty expertise, and readiness for doctoral study. Applicants must satisfy the general admission requirements of the American University of Beirut (AUB) Graduate Council in addition to the program-specific requirements.

Eligibility

Eligibility

A bachelor’s and master’s degree in business administration or a related discipline from a recognized institution for the regular track. Exceptionally qualified applicants holding a bachelor’s degree may be considered for the accelerated track in accordance with AUB regulations.

A minimum GPA of 3.7/4.0, or equivalent, in the most recent degree, subject to AUB and program admissions policies. Exceptional candidates with outstanding research potential may be considered even if they fall slightly below this threshold.

Competitive GMAT or GRE scores obtained within the period accepted by AUB/program regulations.

English-language proficiency meeting AUB requirements.

Evidence of research potential, intellectual maturity, and strong alignment with the research strengths of OSB.

Who Should Apply

Who Should Apply

Applicants may come from business and related fields including economics, finance, accounting, management, marketing, business analytics, operations research, engineering, computer science, statistics, mathematics, information systems, health sciences, and other relevant disciplines. Applicants from other fields may also be considered when they demonstrate strong potential for doctoral research. Preparatory coursework may be required on a case-by-case basis and does not count toward degree requirements.

Application

Application Materials

Applications are submitted through the AUB online graduate application system and should include:

Online Application

Complete the official AUB graduate application form.

Official Transcripts

From all postsecondary institutions attended.

Curriculum Vitae

Highlighting education, research experience, publications or presentations, teaching, professional experience, awards, and scholarships.

Statement of Purpose

Explaining academic background, research interests, motivation for doctoral study, career goals, and fit with OSB faculty expertise.

Research Statement

2–4 pages describing the proposed research area, its significance, preliminary questions, and potential methodological approach.

Writing Sample

Academic writing sample such as a thesis chapter, research paper, working paper, or publication.

Letters of Recommendation

Three academic letters addressing the applicant’s research potential and readiness for doctoral study.

Test Scores

Official GMAT or GRE scores and proof of English-language proficiency, as required.

Interview: Shortlisted applicants will be invited for an interview, during which they will present their proposed research and discuss their academic interests with the admissions committee.

Website note: Link the application deadline, English-language requirements, and official application portal directly to AUB Admissions so that time-sensitive university requirements remain current.

Funding

Financial Support

The PhD in Business Administration is planned as a fully funded, full-time doctoral program. In line with AUB doctoral funding arrangements and program requirements, admitted students are expected to receive tuition support and a monthly stipend in connection with graduate teaching and/or research responsibilities, subject to satisfactory academic progress and the terms of the applicable doctoral appointment.

Additional competitive support may be available for conference participation, research activities, and approved international research experiences.
