Your doctoral journey: from research foundations to an original contribution.

From your first doctoral seminar to your dissertation defense, each stage is designed to help you become an independent scholar.

The program is designed as a full-time research-intensive doctoral experience. Individual programs of study are developed with faculty advisers and integrate research-methods training, doctoral seminars, specialization courses, qualifying examinations, and dissertation research.

Students entering with a master’s degree complete a minimum of 48 credits beyond the master’s degree.

Doctoral Journey

Four years, four stages of becoming a scholar

Each year of the program builds deliberately on the last — from methodological foundations, through specialization and candidacy, to original research and placement.

  1. 01 Year 1

    Research Foundations

    Build your methodological and disciplinary foundations, participate in doctoral seminars, begin working with faculty, and start defining your research interests.

  2. 02 Year 2

    Specialization & Candidacy Preparation

    Deepen your specialization, pursue independent research, prepare for teaching, complete Qualifying Exam Part I/comprehensive examination, and begin defining your thesis direction.

  3. 03 Year 3

    Dissertation Development

    Develop and defend your dissertation proposal, conduct original research, participate in conferences, and begin developing manuscripts for publication.

  4. 04 Year 4

    Dissertation Completion & Placement

    Complete and defend your dissertation, disseminate your research, develop publications, and prepare for the academic job market.

Milestones

Key academic milestones

Students progress through key academic milestones, including:

  • Advanced coursework

    In research methods and their chosen specialization.

  • Qualifying Exam Part I

    Comprehensive examination in the student’s field.

  • Qualifying Exam Part II

    Oral defense of the doctoral thesis proposal.

  • Original dissertation research

    Under faculty supervision.

  • Successful dissertation defense

    Demonstrating an original scholarly contribution.

  • Completion of AUB doctoral degree requirements

    All remaining program and university requirements successfully met.

Mentoring

Research Mentoring & Scholarly Community

Your doctoral research develops through close mentorship, collaboration, and scholarly exchange.

During the early stages of the program, you will receive academic advising to develop your program of study and identify potential research directions. As your research agenda develops, you will work closely with a thesis adviser and doctoral thesis committee.

You will also be an active member of OSB’s scholarly community through faculty research collaboration, research seminars, workshops, visiting-scholar activities, conferences, and opportunities to present work in progress and receive feedback from faculty and peers.

Teaching

Teaching & Academic Career Development

Develop not only as a researcher, but also as an educator and future member of the academic community.

You will complete AUB’s required preparation in teaching in higher education and gain supervised teaching experience and instructional opportunities.

Professional development will also include scholarly writing, conference presentations, publication training, research ethics, networking, and preparation for the international academic job market.

Global Reach

International Research Exposure

Build scholarly connections beyond borders.

You will be encouraged to develop international research networks through conferences, collaborative research, and engagement with visiting and partner scholars.

Subject to academic approval and partnership arrangements, opportunities may include spending a semester at an international partner institution, taking approved courses, and/or advancing your research under complementary academic mentorship.

International placements are subject to academic approval and existing partnership arrangements.
