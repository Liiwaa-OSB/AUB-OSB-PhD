The PhD in Business Administration

A research-intensive doctoral experience designed to develop independent scholars.

The PhD in Business Administration at the Suliman S. Olayan School of Business (OSB), American University of Beirut, is a full-time, fully funded doctoral program designed to prepare exceptional scholars to produce rigorous, relevant, and original research and become future academic leaders.
