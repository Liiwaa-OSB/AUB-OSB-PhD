American University of Beirut Suliman S. Olayan School of Business (OSB)

The PhD in Business Administration at the Suliman S. Olayan School of Business (OSB), American University of Beirut, is a full-time, fully funded, research-intensive doctoral program designed to prepare exceptional scholars to produce rigorous, relevant, and original research and become future academic leaders.

Combining advanced methodological training, disciplinary depth, and close faculty mentorship, the program offers a distinctive platform for investigating important business questions with regional relevance and global significance.
