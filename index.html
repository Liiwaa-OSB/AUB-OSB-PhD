Olayan School of Business (OSB) is a full-time, research-intensive doctoral program that prepares exceptional scholars to produce rigorous, relevant, and original research and to become future academic leaders. Located at the American University of Beirut, the program offers advanced methodological training, disciplinary depth, close faculty mentorship, and a distinctive platform for studying business questions with regional relevance and global significance.

Overview

The PhD in Business Administration

The PhD in Business Administration is designed to prepare the next generation of scholars, educators, and thought leaders in business. Through rigorous coursework, advanced research training, independent inquiry, and sustained faculty mentorship, students develop the methodological expertise, disciplinary depth, and intellectual independence required to make substantive contributions to business knowledge and practice.

Students join OSB’s research community from the beginning of the program, working with faculty on research projects, participating in seminars and workshops, and progressively developing their own research agenda. The program encourages interdisciplinary inquiry and provides a strong foundation for scholarly publication, university teaching, international collaboration, and academic career development.

Graduates will be prepared primarily for tenure-track faculty and research careers at leading universities, while also possessing the advanced analytical and research capabilities sought by research institutes, policy organizations, international organizations, government agencies, consulting firms, and knowledge-intensive organizations.

Learning Outcomes

Program Learning Outcomes

By graduation, PhD candidates are expected to be able to:

  • Demonstrate deep theoretical and empirical knowledge in their field of specialization.
  • Formulate important research questions and design rigorous studies using appropriate quantitative and/or qualitative methodologies.
  • Conduct independent and interdisciplinary research that generates original contributions to business knowledge.
  • Use advanced analytical techniques, research tools, and evidence to address complex business questions.
  • Evaluate the local, regional, and global implications of emerging business research and scientific developments.
  • Develop the capacity to produce rigorous, original high quality oresearch suitable for publication in leading, high-quality international peer-reviewed journals in their field.
  • Communicate research effectively through scholarly writing, conference presentations, teaching, and professional engagement.
  • Demonstrate research integrity, ethical responsibility, collaboration, and commitment to lifelong scholarly development.
Career Outcomes

Career Outcomes

The program is designed primarily for candidates who aspire to academic and research careers. Graduates will be prepared to compete for tenure-track faculty positions and research appointments at universities and research institutions internationally. The program’s advanced research and analytical training also supports careers in international organizations, government and policy institutions, consulting, and research-intensive industry roles.
