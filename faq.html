Frequently Asked Questions

Questions

Everything you need to know

Answers to the questions prospective students ask most often about the PhD in Business Administration.

01 What degree will I earn?

The degree is a PhD in Business Administration. Students pursue one of three doctoral tracks: Business Analytics, Finance, or Management.

02 Is the program full time?

Yes. The PhD is designed as a full-time, research-intensive program requiring sustained participation in coursework, research, teaching-related activities, seminars, and dissertation work.

03 How long does the program take?

The regular track is designed around approximately four years of full-time study, although completion time is governed by AUB doctoral regulations and the student’s research progress.

04 Do I need the GMAT or GRE?

The program proposal supports GMAT or GRE scores. The website should state the test policy applicable to the Fall 2027 admission cycle once formally confirmed and link to AUB Admissions for current requirements.

05 How should I identify a research fit?

Applicants should review the three doctoral tracks and faculty research profiles and use the statement of purpose and research statement to explain alignment with relevant OSB faculty expertise.

06 Is financial support available?

The program is planned as fully funded for admitted full-time doctoral students, with tuition support and a monthly stipend linked to teaching and/or research responsibilities, subject to applicable university and program conditions.

07 Will I teach during the PhD?

Doctoral students complete AUB teaching-development requirements and are expected to contribute to teaching as part of their assistantship and professional preparation.

08 Can I study or conduct research abroad?

The program is designed to encourage international research engagement, including conferences, collaboration, and a possible approved semester at a partner institution, subject to academic and funding arrangements.

09 When can I apply for Fall 2027?

Application dates should be published once the Fall 2027 AUB graduate admissions calendar is finalized. The website should link directly to the official AUB graduate admissions portal and deadlines page.
