Find the faculty whose research connects with your interests and discover where your ideas could take you.

Prospective students are encouraged to explore faculty profiles to identify potential research alignment before applying.

Faculty profiles should highlight research interests, selected recent publications, methodological expertise, and links to full AUB faculty profiles and relevant scholarly profiles.
