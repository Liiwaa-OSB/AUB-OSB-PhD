Faculty and Research Mentorship

The PhD in Business Administration is supported by OSB faculty whose research spans the three doctoral tracks and a wide range of interdisciplinary topics. Faculty members mentor doctoral students from the beginning of the program, guiding their research training, dissertation work, and professional development.

Use the filter below to explore faculty by doctoral track, then reach out to potential advisers whose research aligns with your interests. Faculty profiles include research areas, methodological expertise, recent publications, and links to full AUB profiles.
