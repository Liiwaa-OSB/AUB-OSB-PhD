The Program

A Journey Of Discovery And Impact

The PhD in Business Administration prepares the next generation of scholars, educators, and thought leaders in business. Through rigorous coursework, advanced research training, independent inquiry, and sustained faculty mentorship, students develop the methodological expertise, disciplinary depth, and intellectual independence required to make substantive contributions to business knowledge and practice.

Students join OSB's research community from day one — working with faculty, participating in seminars and workshops, and progressively developing their own research agenda toward a tenure-track academic career.
